Personal Nursing Philosophy

My decision to become a nurse grew from my interest in understanding people through Psychology and was strengthened during my nursing placements, where I saw the difference compassionate nursing care can make during vulnerable moments of a person's life.

I believe nursing is more than completing clinical tasks. It is about building trust, showing compassion, advocating for others and treating every person with dignity and respect.

My practice is guided by person centred care, evidence based practice, Jean Watson's Theory of Human Caring and the principles of Te Tiriti o Waitangi.

I am committed to providing culturally safe care through partnership, protection, participation, options and tino rangatiratanga while supporting equitable health outcomes for Māori and all communities.
